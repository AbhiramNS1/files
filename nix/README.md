### Features Included
- **Personal Finance & Expense Tracker**: Fast offline SQLite tracking, category filtering, monthly/weekly budget analytics, encrypted Google Drive cloud backup.
- **UPI QR Scanner & Payments**: Native camera barcode scanner extracting UPI payment intents and forwarding directly to installed payment apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
- **24-Hour Day Planner & Real-Time Tracker**: Non-overlapping time block architecture, morning kickoff intention setting, interactive timeline, retro fill gaps.
- **Dynamic Real-Time Controls**: Live focus progress banner, +15m quick extend, early completion, and dynamic pivot interruption.
- **Daily Routine Templates**: Custom template creation, routine editing, and one-tap application to any day.
- **Advanced Day Analytics**:
  - *Schedule Fidelity & Plan Adherence Rate*: Punctuality weights, interval overlap similarity, net drift breakdown.
  - *Context Switching & Fragmentation Index*: Category transitions, focus block duration, cognitive flow status.
  - *24-Hour Completeness (Accountability Score)*: Full 1,440-minute tracking, void detection, and categorical life balance.
